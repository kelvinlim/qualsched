Kelvin O. Lim at the University of Minnesota.

Questions: [kolim@umn.edu](mailto:kolim@umn.edu).

## Eligibility

QualSched is for authorized researchers only. You may sign in only with a Google account
that QualSched has allowlisted (an existing researcher row, a bootstrap superadmin
email, or a configured email domain such as `@umn.edu` on the hosted instance). Study
participants do not have accounts here.

You must have a Qualtrics account and API token for the studies you run. QualSched does
not create your survey, mailing list, or message templates.

## Your responsibilities

- Use QualSched only for legitimate research operations you are authorized to perform.
- Keep your Qualtrics API token confidential. QualSched stores it encrypted and never
  sends it to the browser; you must still treat it as a secret.
- Do not attempt to use QualSched to collect, export, or store participant contact PHI
  in this application. Participant records stay in Qualtrics.
- Comply with applicable IRB, HIPAA, FERPA, and University of Minnesota policies for
  your studies.

## How the service works

QualSched computes invitation plans and books or cancels Qualtrics distributions on
your behalf. Qualtrics holds each invitation until its send time. The plan is not
stored in QualSched after the request completes.

Google sign-in proves identity only. QualSched does not keep Google access or refresh
tokens. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy) for what is stored.
